Rationale: MIT is the least restrictive standard licence for the analysis scripts
and is what the README placeholder already suggests. CC BY 4.0 for documents and
aggregated tables requires attribution, which is the only condition the authors
need, and is the licence most journals and repositories (Zenodo, OSF) expect for
supplementary materials. The questionnaire is the authors' own work and can be
released under the same CC BY 4.0 terms.

Note that the HICSS proceedings paper itself is published under the HICSS/ScholarSpace
terms (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0); that licence applies to the paper, not to this repository.
